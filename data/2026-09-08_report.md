## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 50%
**标题:** InterSing: Explicit Interaction Dynamics for 3D Duet Singing Animation and Beyond
**作者:** Yihan Zhou, Zikai Huang, Yuyang Yu
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04903

**相关性说明:** Both are in Computer Graphics and deal with 3D animation/motion generation, but there are key mismatches: (1) The research profile focuses on extracting 3D motion from 2D VIDEO priors using Video Generation Models (Wanxiang/Seedance), while this paper uses AUDIO-driven generation for 3D head animations - different input modality. (2) The research profile explicitly mentions VLM for quality supervision, which is not present in this paper. (3) This paper focuses on duet singing head animations with interaction dynamics, while the profile is about general video-to-3D motion synthesis pipeline. However, it does partially address the pain point of 3D motion generation with temporal consistency and coordination between characters. Uses diffusion models but in a different application context (audio-driven head animation vs video-driven full body motion).

**摘要:** InterSing是一个用于生成二重唱表演中逼真3D头部动画的框架，通过引入交互logits（交互逻辑值）来表示表演者之间的互动强度，并结合弱监督学习和交互感知扩散模型，实现独立动作、协调行为和它们之间平滑过渡的统一多模式生成。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** An Evaluation Framework for Generating Multi-View Images of a Person in a Scene
**作者:** Mahir Majid, Young Kyung Kim, Guillermo Sapiro
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04603

**相关性说明:** While this paper uses diffusion transformers (similar method family), it addresses a different application domain - multi-view IMAGE generation for camera angle changes, not 3D MOTION generation from video. The research profile focuses on human motion synthesis with temporal consistency, while this paper focuses on static multi-view image synthesis. It partially touches on visual feature extraction (HSRD metric) but doesn't address the core pain points of bridging 2D video to 3D motion synthesis or achieving temporal consistency in motion generation.

**摘要:** 本文针对扩散变换器(Diffusion Transformers, DiTs)在生成空间一致的相机角度变化方面的局限性，提出构建合成多视角数据集的解决方案。由于缺乏专门的人类主体多视角训练数据，当前模型难以执行自由形式的提示性相机角度变化。作者提出头部场景旋转差异(Head Scene Rotation Difference, HSRD)指标来定量评估围绕人物的相机运动，通过解耦相机运动与局部头部姿态操作来实现可靠评估。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Temporal Residual Neural Radiance Fields for Monocular Video Dynamic Human Body Reconstruction
**作者:** Tianle Du, Jie Wang, Xiaolong Xie
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04984

**相关性说明:** While this paper deals with dynamic human body reconstruction from video (related to human motion), it focuses on 3D SCENE RECONSTRUCTION and novel view synthesis using NeRF, NOT 3D motion generation/synthesis. The research profile specifically needs: (1) Video generation models like Seedance, (2) VLM for quality control, (3) Motion synthesis pipelines, (4) Bridging 2D video to 3D motion synthesis. This paper uses a completely different method (Temporal Residual NeRF) for a different goal (3D reconstruction/view synthesis). The application domain is different - reconstruction vs motion generation. It has partial relevance in dealing with temporal consistency in human motion video, but misses the core requirements of the research profile.

**摘要:** 本文提出了一种基于时序残差神经辐射场(Temporal Residual Neural Radiance Fields)的动态场景人体3D建模方法，能够有效处理复杂时空信号，实现新视角渲染和新姿态合成。该方法在保持与Anim-NeRF和Neural Body相似精度的同时，实现了近780倍的时间效率提升。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 30%
**标题:** TourPhysics: Bringing Physics to World Models for Exploration and Manipulation from a Single Image
**作者:** Xin Zhang, Yabo Chen, Zixuan Duan
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04911

**相关性说明:** This paper addresses physics-based video world models for exploration and manipulation from single images, which is a different application domain from 3D motion generation/synthesis. While it touches on geometry-based 2D-3D understanding and temporal consistency, it does not focus on extracting 3D visual guidance features for motion synthesis, using VLMs for quality control, or the specific video generation models (Wanxiang/Seedance) mentioned in the profile. The paper is more related to physics simulation and video generation rather than the 3D motion generation pipeline described in the research profile.

**摘要:** TourPhysics是一个在线框架,从单张图像和声明性物理配置初始化,将确定性模拟与视频生成相结合,通过分离模拟器状态、几何证据、生成器控制和外观记忆的角色,实现持久探索和操作.


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** Step Back to Move Forward: Reflection-Aware Preference Optimization for Visual Generation
**作者:** Junlong Wu, Jiuzhou Lin, Jia Sun
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04282

**相关性说明:** This paper focuses on 2D image generation (T2I) and video generation (T2V) using diffusion models with preference optimization, while the research profile specifically requires 3D motion generation from 2D video priors. Although both use diffusion models, the application domains are completely different - this paper is about visual content generation (2D images/videos), not 3D motion synthesis. The research profile needs methods for extracting 3D visual guidance features from 2D video and bridging the 2D-3D domain gap for motion generation, which this paper does not address at all. The paper mentions T2V (text-to-video) but this is for generating 2D video content, not for extracting 3D motion from video priors.

**摘要:** 本文提出RA-GRPO框架，通过引入扩散反射和反事实路径合成两个创新机制，将"反向"反思融入"正向"生成过程，有效解决了扩散模型强化学习微调中的局部最优和探索效率问题。
