Main category: cs.CV

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 对齐自由的RGB-T显著目标检测无需昂贵的预对齐步骤，但空间不对齐会破坏像素对应关系并导致跨模态融合时的特征污染。

Method: 提出语义到空间对齐框架S2A，包含三个模块：1) GGHF全局引导层次融合模块利用全局语义指导抑制背景干扰；2) AFCA对齐自由跨模态通道注意力模块通过通道交互克服局部空间不对齐；3) SDCA空间可变形交叉注意力模块预测自适应采样偏移恢复局部空间对应。

Result: S2A在多个公共对齐自由RGB-T基准数据集上达到高度竞争力的性能，有效缓解了不对齐导致的特征污染问题。

Conclusion: 通过语义到空间的对齐范式，S2A先实现可靠的跨模态语义交互，再执行局部空间校准，有效降低了特征污染，无需额外技巧即可取得优异表现。

Specifically, a global-guided hierarchical fusion module (GGHF) first exploits global semantic guidance to suppress background interference and refine hierarchical intra-modal features. Subsequently, the alignment-free cross-modal channel attention module (AFCA) globally exchanges complementary semantic information through channel-wise interaction, effectively overcoming the interference caused by local spatial misalignments. Finally, a spatial deformable cross-attention module (SDCA) predicts adaptive sampling offsets to recover local cross-modal spatial correspondence. Through this semantic-to-spatial paradigm, S2A first enables reliable cross-modal semantic interaction and subsequently performs local spatial calibration, effectively reducing misalignment-induced feature contamination. Without bells and whistles, S2A achieves highly competitive performance on multiple public alignment-free RGB-T benchmarks, demonstrating its effectiveness in alleviating misalignment-induced feature contamination.

### [34] [Latent evolving World Action Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27455)
*Xueji Fang,Boqiang Duan,Hua Wu,Jingdong Wang,Guo-Jun Qi*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出LeWAM，一种无需视频扩散模型的高效世界动作模型，通过JEPA预测性嵌入替代VAE压缩 latent进行动作生成，并引入DemoDPO离线偏好优化方法，在仅0.4B参数下达到92.28%的成功率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当前VDM-based WAMs依赖大规模视频生成预训练，导致训练成本高、效率低；且模仿学习无法区分动作质量好坏，需要额外环境交互或人工监督来改进策略。

Method: 1)对比不同视觉表示（VAE latent vs JEPA embeddings），发现JEPA预测性嵌入更适合动作生成；2)提出LeWAM：基于JEPA embeddings条件生成动作，预测未来embedding建模环境演化，移除视频扩散骨干；3)提出DemoDPO：从演示中直接提取偏好监督进行离线优化，无需额外环境交互。

Result: LeWAM在RoboTwin 2.0上达到92.28%平均成功率，与SOTA VLAs和WAMs相当，且在真实世界操作任务中保持有效性。

Conclusion: JEPA预测性嵌入比VAE压缩latent更适合WAM动作生成；DemoDPO可有效提升动作质量而无需环境交互或人工干预；LeWAM以0.4B参数实现高效高性能的world action model。

Abstract: World Action Models (WAMs) jointly model action generation and environment dynamics and are mostly built on pretrained Video Diffusion Models (VDMs). In VDM-based WAMs, observations are first encoded by a VAE, and the resulting compressed latents are then processed by large video diffusion backbones to extract effective features for action generation. However, this paradigm ties WAM performance and training cost to large-scale video generation pretraining, limiting WAM efficiency and scalability. In this paper, we theoretically and empirically investigate how visual representations affect action generation in WAMs. Our results show that predictive embeddings from Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA) encoders better support action generation than compressed VAE latents, with I-JEPA performing best in our encoder comparison. Based on these findings, we propose LeWAM, which conditions action generation on JEPA embeddings and models environment evolution by predicting future embeddings in the same space, without relying on a video diffusion backbone. We further find that imitation learning matches demonstrated actions but does not distinguish better actions from worse ones, even though small action deviations can greatly affect task success. To address this limitation without additional environment interaction or the human oversight required for resets and safety, we introduce Demonstration-Guided DPO (DemoDPO), an offline preference refinement stage that derives preference supervision directly from demonstrations. With only 0.4B trainable parameters, LeWAM achieves an average success rate of 92.28\% on RoboTwin 2.0, comparable to that of state-of-the-art VLAs and WAMs, and maintains practical effectiveness on real-world manipulation tasks.

### [52] [SynSeq: End-to-End SYNTAX Score Prediction from Coronary Angiography Videos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27696)
*Christoph Baumann,Ronny Schweitzer,Noemi Pavo,Ulrike Attenberger,Christian Loewe,Philipp Seeböck*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: SynSeq是一种基于冠脉造影视频直接预测SYNTAX评分的视频方法，通过针对性预处理和零膨胀感知损失函数及线性目标缩放的定制训练策略，在CardioSyntax数据集上显著优于现有方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: SYNTAX评分是评估冠状动脉疾病和指导血运重建治疗决策的重要工具，但手动估计耗时且存在观察者间差异。现有机器学习主要关注病变检测或二元疾病分类，直接SYNTAX评分预测相对空白。

Method: 提出SynSeq方法：结合针对性预处理与定制训练策略，包括零膨胀感知损失函数和线性目标缩放，直接从冠脉造影视频预测SYNTAX评分。

Result: 在CardioSyntax公开数据集上评估：R²提升0.55，预测偏差降低93.1%，三位独立专家标注的一致性更好；血运重建治疗建议的加权F1分数达0.80，略低于专家间一致性。

Conclusion: SynSeq可提供一致、自动化SYNTAX评分评估，为冠状动脉血运重建规划提供可靠决策支持。

Abstract: The SYNTAX score is an established tool for assessing coronary artery disease and guiding revascularization treatment decisions. However, its manual estimation from coronary angiography videos by clinical experts is time-consuming and subject to inter-reader variability. While machine learning has shown promise in automating this process, prior work has primarily focused on lesion detection, characterization, or binary disease classification, leaving direct SYNTAX score prediction relatively unexplored. We propose SynSeq, a video-based method for direct SYNTAX score prediction. It combines targeted preprocessing with a tailored training strategy using a zero-inflation-aware loss and linear target scaling. Evaluated on the public CardioSyntax dataset, SynSeq significantly outperforms previous state-of-the-art methods, improving $R^2$ by 0.55, reducing prediction bias by 93.1% and achieving more consistent performance across annotations from three independent expert graders. In addition, SynSeq achieves a weighted $F_1$-score of 0.80 for revascularization treatment recommendations, slightly below inter-expert agreement. These results demonstrate the potential of SynSeq to provide consistent, automated SYNTAX score assessment and reliable decision support for coronary revascularization planning.

### [147] [CART: Closed-Loop Adaptive Red Teaming for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27336)
*Dongdong Zhang,Tengchao Lv,Yilin Jia,Yuzhong Zhao,Yupan Huang,Wenshan Wu,Xiangyang Zhou,Shaohan Huang,Nan Yang,Li Dong,Lei Cui,Furu Wei*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: CART（闭环自适应红队）是一个自适应框架，通过利用每次测试结果来指导下一步测试，从而克服了固定提示重放方法的局限性。它包含三个独立角色：创建测试的Challenger、被测试的Target（文本模型或工具使用代理），以及评估结果的Judge。该框架在三个评估系列（Frontier、JAH和Agentic）中，相比静态种子重放发现了更多失败案例和更高平均风险，尤其在工具中介代理测试中表现突出。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有自动化红队方法通常重放固定提示集，只能测量已知风险，无法从测试过程中发现的失败中学习。需要一种能够自适应学习、持续发现模型和代理弱点的框架。

Method: CART框架包含四个核心策略：1）从广泛风险覆盖开始；2）跟踪发现的弱点；3）保持新探测的多样性；4）记录每个发现的证据和来源。框架分离了三个角色：Challenger（创建测试）、Target（被测试对象）和Judge（评估结果），允许独立研究这些角色。

Result: 在三个评估系列（Frontier、JAH和Agentic）中，CART相比静态种子重放为每个有可用基线的Target发现了更多失败和更高平均风险。收益扩展到工具介导的代理测试，表明上下文适应性可以揭示直接提示重放无法发现的弱点。Challenger-Judge的选择影响发现的证据，突出了角色分离和独立审查的必要性。

Conclusion: CART将红队从一次性检查清单转变为持续、自适应和可审计的模型与代理弱点搜索。框架证明了角色分离的重要性，以及上下文适应性在发现传统方法无法触及的弱点方面的价值。

Abstract: Automated red teaming often replays a fixed set of prompts, which measures known risks but cannot learn from failures found during testing. We present CART (Closed-Loop Adaptive Red Teaming), a framework that uses each result to guide what it tests next. CART begins with broad risk coverage, follows weaknesses that emerge, keeps new probes diverse, and records the evidence and source of every finding. It separates the Challenger that creates tests, the Target being tested, which may be a text-only model or a bounded tool-using agent, and the Judge that evaluates the results, allowing these roles to be studied independently. Across three evaluation families (Frontier, JAH, and Agentic), CART discovers more failures and higher average risk than static seed replay for every Target with an available baseline. The gains extend to tool-mediated agent tests, suggesting that contextual adaptation can reveal weaknesses that direct prompt replay does not exercise. These results describe what the test policies discover, not how often failures occur in real deployments. We also find that Challenger-Judge choices affect the evidence uncovered, highlighting the need for role separation and independent review. Overall, CART turns red teaming from a one-time checklist into a continuous, adaptive, and auditable search for model and agent weaknesses.
