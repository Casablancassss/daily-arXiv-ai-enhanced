## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 45%
**标题:** When the Judge Acts: Auditing VLM-Guided Image Selection on Culturally Situated Prompts
**作者:** Huichan Seo
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01243

**相关性说明:** This paper addresses quality assessment using VLMs, which tangentially relates to one pain point (quality control of video generation outputs). However, it is fundamentally about VLM-guided IMAGE selection and decision-making biases, NOT about 3D motion generation or text-to-motion synthesis. The application domain is completely different - it's focused on image/video quality evaluation in cultural contexts, not on computer graphics motion generation. While it shares the use of VLMs with the research profile, the end task (image selection vs. 3D motion synthesis) is entirely different. The paper does not address extracting 3D visual guidance features, ensuring 2D-3D consistency, or high-quality motion synthesis from visual inputs.

**摘要:** 研究对VLM作为图像选择评判者进行审计，发现4B参数模型存在严重的位置偏差（49%选择第一张图像），决策容易被候选顺序影响，且表现不如CLIP相似度基线；8B模型表现更好但针对4B模型的过滤器反而会损害其决策。滤波器需要针对具体模型重新审计。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** PACT: End-to-End Learning of Human Pose, Contacts, and Forces from Video
**作者:** Rikhat Akizhanov, Yangsong Zhang, Nikolai Kaliazin
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00451

**相关性说明:** While this paper deals with human pose estimation from video, it's focused on pose/force ANALYSIS and estimation rather than motion SYNTHESIS or generation. The research profile is specifically about Text-to-Motion (generating 3D motion from text/video), while PACT is about estimating pose and contact forces from existing video - this is a different application domain (analysis vs generation). PACT doesn't use video generation models, VLMs, or motion generation methods mentioned in the profile. It's a pose estimation and physical interaction analysis method, not a motion synthesis method.

**摘要:** PACT是一个端到端模型,能从单目视频中联合估计人体姿态、接触点和接触力。该方法通过在人体重建基础模型上添加可学习的接触力标记和时间transformer,结合物理监督来处理力标注稀缺的问题,并构建了ForceWall爬墙基准数据集。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** VisionQ: VLM-as-a-Judge Taxonomy, Dataset and Benchmark for Qualitative Analysis in Computer Vision
**作者:** Vu Dinh Xuan, Duc-Hai Nguyen, Minh-Dung Dao
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00666

**相关性说明:** Uses VLM (a method in profile) but wrong application domain. VisionQ focuses on evaluating visual reasoning quality of VLMs in general Computer Vision (assessing image comparisons), not on Text-to-Motion, 3D motion generation, or video-to-motion synthesis. While it addresses quality assessment (one pain point), it's about evaluating VLM judgments rather than using VLMs to guide 3D motion generation from video. The application domain is fundamentally different - general CV quality assessment vs. computer graphics motion synthesis.

**摘要:** VisionQ是首个基于同行评审CV比较图的基准测试，通过将每个判断锚定在命名视觉标准上，评估视觉-语言模型在定性比较中的视觉推理能力。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Bootstrapping Video Interaction Generation with Synthetic State Transitions
**作者:** Jiho Jang, Jinyoung Kim, Nojun Kwak
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01039

**相关性说明:** While this paper deals with video generation and mentions visual anchors for state transitions, it focuses on VIDEO GENERATION for physical interactions (robotics/VR/AR applications), NOT 3D Motion Generation from visual inputs. The research profile specifically needs methods for Text-to-Motion, extracting 3D guidance features from 2D video, and 3D motion synthesis. This paper addresses generating video of interactions rather than extracting/generating 3D human motion - these are different application domains despite some overlap in methods (video generation, quality assessment). The pain points being solved are different: this paper addresses video generation quality for physical interactions, not the specific challenges of 3D motion synthesis from visual priors.

**摘要:** 该研究提出一个生成可控交互合成数据集的框架，通过结构化分类法和图像编辑模型创建起止状态图像作为视觉锚点，并开发状态引导采样(SGS)技术和自动化评估系统，以提升视频生成模型对物理交互和状态转换的建模能力。


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** EgoFound3R: End-to-End Egocentric Hand Reconstruction in World Space with Point-Wise Interaction Attributes
**作者:** Hongming Fu, Jingcheng Shi, Wenjia Wang
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01210

**相关性说明:** The paper is about 3D hand reconstruction from egocentric videos, which is a computer vision / 3D reconstruction task, NOT Computer Graphics / Text-to-Motion generation. While both deal with 3D motion from visual inputs, the application domain is fundamentally different: this is about reconstructing existing hand poses from video (estimation), not generating new motion sequences from text or video prompts (synthesis). The methods are also unrelated - no diffusion models, video generation models, or motion generation techniques from the research profile are used. It only partially addresses one pain point (extracting 3D guidance from 2D video) but misses the core focus on video generation quality control and text-to-motion synthesis.

**摘要:** EgoFound3R是一个统一的端到端模型，用于从第一人称视频中重建世界坐标系中的手部几何形状，并预测点级交互属性（可见性、接触、距离），相比现有方法将MPJPE降低11.6%-43.2%，同时吞吐量提升约6倍。
