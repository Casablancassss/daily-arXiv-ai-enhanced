## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 30%
**标题:** ViRDM: Taming Representation Distribution Matching for Few-Step Causal Video Generation
**作者:** Zichong Meng, Chongjian Ge, Chun-Hao P. Huang
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28923

**相关性说明:** The paper focuses on general causal VIDEO GENERATION (2D video synthesis), while the research profile is about 3D MOTION GENERATION (mapping 2D video to 3D human motion). These are DIFFERENT application domains in computer graphics. Although both involve "video generation," the paper addresses general video content creation (like text-to-video), NOT 3D human motion generation from 2D inputs. The pain points about "二维视频到三维动作的几何映射失真" and "从二维视频中提取可靠的三维几何引导特征" are completely unaddressed. The paper mentions "temporal dynamics" but refers to video quality, not physical plausibility of 3D motion. Methodologically, this uses video diffusion models, not 3D motion generation models. This is a case of using similar methods (diffusion) in completely different application domains.

**摘要:** ViRDM是一种无需教师和评论家的视频后训练方法，用于少步骤自回归视频扩散生成。通过将图像表示分布匹配(RDM)迁移到视频生成，解决了内存不可行的梯度路径、独特的视频优化 regime 和表征分布对时间动态约束不足三个关键障碍。结合截断清洁退出监督、轻量级VAE解码器和分阶段向量-雅可比积等技术，仅需20次生成器更新即可在VBench上达到84.87分。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** DeltaWAM: Delta World Action Models for Bimanual Manipulation
**作者:** Han Yan, Zishang Xiang, Haokai Jiang
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28811

**相关性说明:** The paper is about ROBOTIC manipulation control (robotics), not Computer Graphics. While both deal with motion, the application domain is completely different: the research profile focuses on 2D video to 3D human motion generation for graphics/animation, while this paper focuses on robot control using world models. This is a robotics paper that happens to use some similar concepts (visual prediction, motion), but the end goal and domain are entirely different. Using "world models" for robot control is not comparable to "video generation models" or "3D motion generation models" for graphics content creation.

**摘要:** DeltaWAM是一种改进的世界模型，通过预测视觉变化而非完整未来帧来提升机器人控制效率，同时引入流式Delta Memory减少推理计算成本。
