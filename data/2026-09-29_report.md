# 📊 每日 arXiv 论文推荐

**研究画像:**
- 领域: Computer Graphics - Text-to-Motion Generation
- 方法: Vision Language Model (VLM), Video Generation Models (Wan Xiang, Seedance), 2D-to-3D Motion Extraction, Visual Feature Guidance, Downstream 3D Motion Generation Models, Quality Supervision and Filtering
- 痛点: Quality control of generated 2D videos as reliable priors, Effective extraction of visual guidance features from 2D video for 3D motion generation, Bridging the domain gap between 2D visual representations and 3D motion space, Ensuring temporal consistency and motion realism in synthesis, Managing noise and artifacts in video-to-motion translation

## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 55%
**标题:** Timo: $\textbf{T}$aming Mult$\textbf{i}$modal Diffusion Transformer for Human $\textbf{Mo}$tion Generation
**作者:** Zhao Wang, Jiangtao Hu, Jack Yu
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30761

**相关性说明:** The paper is about human motion generation (application domain match), but it focuses on TEXT-to-Motion generation while the research profile specifically requires VIDEO-to-Motion generation with VLM, video generation models, and 2D-to-3D motion extraction. Timo uses text captions as input, not 2D video. While both deal with motion generation, the input modality is fundamentally different - this paper doesn't address extracting guidance from 2D video or bridging 2D visual representations to 3D motion space, which are core pain points in the profile.

**摘要:** 提出Timo框架，解决人体动作生成中文本与动作双向建模不足的问题，通过运动学感知的MMDiT结合流匹配和几何旋转运动学监督，显著超越现有方法。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 45%
**标题:** Motion Style Slider: Endpoint-Supervised Continuous Style Control for Human Motion Diffusion
**作者:** Chen-Chieh Liao, Yichen Peng, Yiyi Cai
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30795

**相关性说明:** The paper uses diffusion for human motion generation (same general domain), but focuses on motion-to-motion style transfer rather than text-to-motion or video-to-motion generation. The research profile specifically requires 2D video-to-3D motion extraction, VLM-based approaches, and visual feature guidance from 2D videos - none of which are addressed by this paper on style control between existing motions. This is a different sub-domain within motion generation.

**摘要:** 本文提出Motion Style Slider框架，实现动作到动作的风格迁移，并通过标量强度实现细粒度连续控制。该方法在学习的动作-风格嵌入空间中构建风格方向，结合扩散去噪与潜在强度正则化，实现平滑单调的风格缩放。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Where Compute Matters: Heterogeneous Attention for Efficient Video Diffusion
**作者:** Olga Zatsarynna, Denis Korzhenkov, Juergen Gall
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31050

**相关性说明:** The paper is about efficient video diffusion for 2D VIDEO GENERATION, not text-to-motion generation. While it mentions Wan Xiang video generation model and uses diffusion methods, the application domain is fundamentally different - video generation creates 2D visual content while motion generation creates 3D skeletal movements. The paper addresses computational efficiency in video generation, not the 2D-to-3D motion extraction, visual guidance feature extraction, or domain gap bridging that the research profile requires. It uses similar underlying diffusion technology but targets a completely different output modality.

**摘要:** HetA-DiT是一种用于高效视频生成的非均匀注意力机制，通过根据token难度自适应分配计算资源来解决自注意力的二次复杂度问题。该方法引入一个轻量级不确定性分支预测token级别的去噪难度，将不确定token路由到密集全局注意力，而将可靠token处理为高效局部注意力，在VBench等基准上仅需约20%token通过密集注意力即可保持竞争性生成质量。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** Action Forcing: Training World Models on Unsupervised Video by Recovering Underlying Egomotion Bases
**作者:** Ashish Sundar, Tiankuo Hou, Zhong Fan
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30595

**相关性说明:** This paper focuses on training world models for robotics/ego-motion from unsupervised video (extracting throttle-yaw control signals from vehicle/robot egomotion), which is a completely different application domain from Text-to-Motion Generation in Computer Graphics. While it deals with extracting motion signals from video, the target is robot vehicle control, not human 3D motion synthesis. The methods (PCA on pixel displacements for ego-motion bases, unsupervised video learning) are unrelated to the research profile's methods (VLM, video generation models like Wan Xiang/Seedance, 2D-to-3D motion extraction for human motion, visual feature guidance). This is a case of using similar general concepts (motion extraction from video) but in a fundamentally different domain (robotics vs. computer graphics human motion).

**摘要:** 本文提出从普通无标签视频中恢复动作监督训练数据的方法，通过数据驱动的自我运动基座获取控制信号，并引入无参考评估方法来衡量世界模型的可控性、 plausibility、conjuring和几何完整性。


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** MVVBench: Benchmarking 4D Reasoning in Vision-Language Models
**作者:** Hyungjin Chung, Byeongjun Park, Joonseok Lee
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30952

**相关性说明:** This paper is about multi-view video reasoning and 4D reasoning in Vision-Language Models, which is a different application domain from Text-to-Motion Generation in Computer Graphics. While both involve VLMs, this paper focuses on video understanding and question answering, not motion synthesis or generation. The paper addresses reasoning across multiple camera views and time, not extracting motion from video or generating 3D motion. The application domain mismatch is critical - this is video understanding/reasoning, not motion generation.

**摘要:** MVVBench是一个用于评估多视角视频推理的基准测试数据集，专注于4D多视角推理能力，即整合空间和时间信息来回答仅凭单一视角或单一时刻无法解答的问题。
