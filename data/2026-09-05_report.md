# 📊 每日 arXiv 论文推荐

**研究画像:**
- 领域: Computer Graphics / 3D Human Motion Generation
- 方法: Visual Language Model (VLM), Video Generation Models (万相/Seedance), Visual Feature Extraction, 3D Motion Generation Model, Quality Supervision
- 痛点: 高质量三维动作合成的数据稀缺问题, 文本到动作映射的语义保真度不足, 二维视频先验信息到三维空间的转换精度, 动作质量的自动评估与筛选机制缺失

**最相关的 5 篇论文:**


## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 30%
**标题:** VeriPhy: Agentic Physical Reasoning for World Model Evaluation and Refinement
**作者:** Wenzhuo Xu, Yuchen Zhu, Chongjian Ge
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03153

**相关性说明:** This paper focuses on physical reasoning and verification for video generation (detecting physical errors in videos), which is fundamentally different from 3D Human Motion Generation. While it touches on quality evaluation (related to one pain point about motion quality assessment), it does not address the core challenges of 3D human motion synthesis - data scarcity, text-to-motion semantic fidelity, or 2D-to-3D conversion. The methods (VLMs, video analysis) are used for physical verification in video generation, not for generating or evaluating 3D human motions. The application domain mismatch is significant - video physics verification vs. computer graphics human motion generation.

**摘要:** VeriPhy是一个可审计的物理验证系统，用于检测生成视频中的物理错误。现有视频质量评分无法指出具体违反了哪些物理规则或在何时违反。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 30%
**标题:** WorldReward: Reward Modeling for Camera-Conditioned World Models
**作者:** Yibin Wang, Zehan Wang, Junshu Tang
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03952

**相关性说明:** This paper focuses on camera-conditioned world models for robotics/simulation (WorldPlay benchmarks), not 3D human motion generation in computer graphics. While it uses VLMs and addresses motion quality evaluation (partially matching one pain point), the application domain is fundamentally different. The paper evaluates generated videos from world models, not human motion synthesis. Methods for human motion generation (text-to-motion, 3D motion models) are not addressed.

**摘要:** WorldReward是一个基于视觉语言模型的成对偏好奖励模型，用于统一评估相机条件世界模型的动作一致性和视觉质量。该方法将配对视频分解为动作对齐的片段，通过结构化视觉证据组织，利用投票机制聚合片段级决策。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** Mudragen: Geometrically Supervised Generation of Interacting Two-Hand Mudras for Preserving Indian Classical Dance Heritage
**作者:** Jagadish Kashinath Kamble, Jayanta Mukhopadhyay, Debaditya Roy
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03415

**相关性说明:** This paper focuses on 2D RGB IMAGE generation of hand gestures (mudras), not 3D HUMAN MOTION GENERATION. While both use diffusion models and involve human poses, the application domain is fundamentally different - static 2D image synthesis vs. temporal 3D motion sequence generation. The paper addresses geometric supervision for image generation but does not tackle 3D motion synthesis, text-to-motion mapping, 2D-to-3D conversion, or motion quality evaluation - all key pain points in the research profile.

**摘要:** MudraGen是一个条件扩散框架，用于合成Samyukta Hasta Mudras（印度古典舞Bharatanatyam中的双手互动手势）的逼真RGB图像。该框架引入几何感知监督来解决双手协调、解剖学有效性和文化细微差别问题。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** Building Pretraining Data for World Models: An Unreal Engine-Based Pipeline for Action-Conditioned Video Generation
**作者:** Haoyu Wang, Songchun Zhang, Haoran Li
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03557

**相关性说明:** 该论文是关于使用Unreal Engine生成动作条件化视频数据用于世界模型预训练，与研究profile要求的"三维人体动作生成"应用领域不同。论文生成的是2D视频数据而非3D动作数据。虽然涉及数据稀缺问题，但针对的是视频生成而非动作生成；未使用VLM、视频生成模型、视觉特征提取或3D动作生成模型。该工作更接近游戏引擎/视频生成领域，而非计算机图形学中的3D人体动作生成。

**摘要:** 本文介绍了一个基于Unreal Engine的大规模合成数据生产管道，用于生成动作条件化的多视角视频。该管道采用两阶段架构解决了实时物理模拟与高质量离线渲染的不同执行需求，生产了超过8,700小时的视频数据，并已应用于EchoWM世界模型。


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** The Shape of Time: Video-Token Contrast for Temporal Understanding in VideoLMs
**作者:** Yumeng Shi, Quanyu Long, Yin Wu
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04110

**相关性说明:** The paper focuses on Video Language Models (VideoLM) for temporal understanding in video understanding tasks, which is a completely different application domain from 3D Human Motion Generation. While the profile mentions VLM as a method, this paper uses VLM for video question-answering/temporal reasoning, not for generating 3D human motions. The paper addresses how VideoLMs can learn temporal dynamics through video token contrast, but this has nothing to do with synthesizing 3D human motions, converting 2D video priors to 3D space, or motion quality evaluation - which are the core pain points in the research profile. This is a case of using similar methods (VLM) in entirely different application domains.

**摘要:** VT-Contrast通过在视频Token表示层面引入时序监督，解决VideoLM模型依赖表层线索（物体、场景、语言先验）而忽视事件动态的问题。该方法通过对比保持顺序的视频视图与重排序的替代视图（基于Kendall tau距离），监督晚期层的后一帧视频Token，在无需改变模型架构的情况下提升时序理解能力。
