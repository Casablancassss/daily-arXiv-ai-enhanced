## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Scanline-Aware Animatable Gaussian Avatars from Rolling-Shutter Videos
**作者:** Youxiang Wang
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17314

**相关性说明:** The paper addresses 3D human avatars but focuses on reconstruction from rolling-shutter video, not text-to-motion generation. It's about fixing video capture artifacts in Gaussian avatar reconstruction, which differs significantly from the research profile's focus on VLMs and video generation models for generating new 3D motions from 2D inputs. The application domain is avatar reconstruction/renewal rather than motion synthesis from textual or visual guidance.

**摘要:** RS-Avatar通过在扫描线级别建模rolling-shutter效应，从rolling-shutter视频中重建清晰、无失真的可动画化3D高斯头像，解决了多视角视频中不同扫描线捕获不同时间姿态导致的剪切和晃动问题。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 30%
**标题:** SemComp-Bench: Benchmarking Semantic Task Completion in Video Generation
**作者:** Keyu Tu, Zhuowei Chen, Mengqi Huang
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17426

**相关性说明:** This paper is about semantic task completion in general video generation (benchmarking whether videos successfully complete tasks), NOT about 3D human motion generation. While it uses VLM for evaluation, the application domain is completely different - it's focused on video generation quality assessment, not text-to-motion or 3D human motion synthesis. The pain points in the profile (extracting 3D motion from 2D, bridging 2D-3D domain gap, temporal consistency in motions) are not addressed. Only the shared use of VLM creates a superficial method similarity, but the core application domain mismatch makes this largely irrelevant to the research profile.

**摘要:** 提出语义任务完成视频生成(Semantic Task Completion Video Generation)这一新的结果导向的视频生成任务,重点关注任务结果的实现而非中间步骤或表面一致性。构建了SemComp-Data评估数据集和SemComp-Bench评估协议。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** Structural Plan-to-Model Conversion with Deterministic Geometry and Guarded Agentic Vision-Language Refinement
**作者:** Mohammad Talebi-Kalaleh, Qipei Mei
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17237

**相关性说明:** The paper uses VLM (Vision Language Model) which matches one method in the research profile, but the APPLICATION DOMAIN is completely different. This paper is about structural engineering - converting architectural/structural drawings (PDFs) to finite element 3D models for building construction. The research profile is specifically about Computer Graphics, Text-to-Motion, and 3D Human Motion Generation (e.g., generating human motion from video). This is not about human motion generation at all - it's about structural engineering component detection. While both deal with 2D-to-3D conversion, the domain (architectural structures vs human motion) and purpose are entirely different. Using VLM for structural component detection in architectural drawings is not comparable to using VLM for human motion generation.

**摘要:** 首次提出将代理视觉语言层应用于框架图纸PDF中的结构构件检测和模型草图绘制，无需针对特定任务的检测器训练或微调。框架包含确定性阶段（提取图元、通过尺寸比例共识估计比例、识别五种实体类、组装可编辑布局）和代理阶段（提出受约束的类型修正）。在100张图纸的基准测试中，比例估计精度达到参考值的0.1%以内，柱、梁、墙、支撑、开洞的召回率/精确率分别为0.922/0.997、0.886/0.990、1.000/1.000、1.000/1.000、1.000/0.964。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 15%
**标题:** PXDepth: Pixel-Space Modeling for Structure Preserving Monocular Depth Estimation
**作者:** Zhiyuan Yuan, Guanying Chen, Lingteng Qiu
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16984

**相关性说明:** This paper is about monocular depth estimation from 2D images, which is a completely different application domain from Text-to-Motion and 3D Human Motion Generation. While both fields fall under computer vision broadly, this paper addresses estimating depth/surface structure from single images - not generating 3D human motion from text or video. The methods (ViT for depth prediction) and goals (geometric depth estimation vs. motion synthesis) are fundamentally different. This does not address any of the stated pain points about extracting 3D motion from video, VLM quality control, or temporal consistency in motion synthesis.

**摘要:** PXDepth提出一种区分性的单目深度估计模型，通过分离全局上下文建模和像素级深度预测来解决现有方法中细粒度结构丢失的问题。


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 15%

**作者:** Darshan Nagendra Prasad, Lars Ullrich, Knut Graichen
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17095

**相关性说明:** Complete domain mismatch. This paper is about autonomous driving (Vision-Language-Action models for vehicle control) while the research profile is about Computer Graphics, Text-to-Motion, and 3D Human Motion Generation. Although both use Vision Language Models, the application domains are fundamentally different - this paper focuses on driving behavior and traffic scenarios, not human motion synthesis for graphics/animation. The pain points, methods, and end goals are entirely different.

**摘要:** 研究提出一种无需重新训练的视觉-语言-动作(VLA)驾驶模型注意力重定向方法，通过在推理阶段对安全关键行为人的视觉token施加有界加性注意力偏置，使模型将注意力转向指定目标，同时保持因果链推理路径不变。
