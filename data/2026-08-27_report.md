## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 50%
**标题:** InteractGesture: Progressive Chunk Guidance for Continuous Streaming Co-Speech Gesture Control
**作者:** Ekkasit Pinyoanuntapong, Ajinkya Deogade, Paul Streli
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25734

**相关性说明:** While this paper uses diffusion models for 3D motion generation (matching one method), it focuses specifically on speech-driven co-speech gesture generation - a specific type of human motion. The research profile emphasizes video-to-3D motion transformation with VLM supervision and visual guidance feature extraction from 2D video, which this gesture generation paper does not address. It partially matches the "Ensuring quality and consistency of generated motions" pain point but misses the core video-to-3D pipeline and VLM integration aspects central to the research profile.

**摘要:** 本文提出了InteractGesture，一个模型无关的推理时方法，用于实现可空间控制的语音伴随手势生成。该方法通过可微分的RVQ-VAE解码器引导扩散采样器的目标潜在估计，通过反向传播空间控制梯度来调整采样过程中的运动潜在变量。同时提出渐进分块引导策略，解决流式生成中分块依赖导致的边界不一致问题。在BEAT2数据集上的实验表明，该方法在保持手势整体质量的同时提升了多关节空间控制能力。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** 4DStreamCtrl: Interactive Video Generation with Online 4D Control
**作者:** Shiqian Li, Chenguo Lin, Zhiguang Liu
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25479

**相关性说明:** This paper focuses on video generation (2D video frames with camera/object control), not 3D motion generation for characters or robots. While it uses diffusion models similar to the research profile, the application domain is fundamentally different. The research profile targets video-to-3D motion transformation and motion generation from visual guidance, whereas this paper targets controllable video generation for visual effects. The methods (unified 3D point trajectories for camera/object control) don't address the pain points of extracting 3D guidance features for motion generation or VLM supervision for motion generation.

**摘要:** 4DStreamCtrl是一个统一的3D点轨迹表示框架,将相机运动、物体轨迹和深度融合到一个接口中,实现联合相机和物体控制、深度编辑和运动迁移的实时流式视频生成。该模型在单个高端GPU上以20 FPS运行,可生成数百帧 temporally coherent的视频。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 30%
**标题:** V-Link: Recovering Lost Visual Representations in Action DiT for Vision-Language-Action Models
**作者:** Yehao Lu, Jiarui Yang, Yuning Su
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25308

**相关性说明:** While the paper uses VLM and diffusion models (similar methods), it addresses ROBOTICS/robot manipulation rather than Computer Graphics 3D motion generation. The application domain is fundamentally different - this is about controlling robots (real-world manipulation tasks like picking and placing), not generating 3D human motion from video. The pain points being solved are about robot visual grounding, not video-to-3D motion transformation or quality control in motion generation. Despite sharing some technical components (VLM, DiT), the core application and research goal are misaligned with the research profile.

**摘要:** V-Link是一种新型视觉-语言-动作(VLA)模型架构，通过在视觉语言模型(VLM)到动作(DiT)的特征传输过程中恢复视觉表示来解决VLA架构中动作专家对3D几何和2D语义信息访问受限的问题。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 30%
**标题:** VBVR-Pro: A Scalable and Verifiable Suite for Native Visual Reasoning
**作者:** Junxiang Xu, Ruisi Wang, Fanyi Pu
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26105

**相关性说明:** The paper is about visual reasoning benchmarks and evaluating video/image generation models for reasoning tasks, while the research profile focuses on Computer Graphics for 3D motion generation from video. Although both involve video generation, the application domains are fundamentally different: the paper deals with visual reasoning tasks (tracking, understanding visual states), not 3D motion generation or extracting 3D visual guidance features. The pain points are completely different - this paper addresses reasoning evaluation, not video-to-3D motion transformation quality control.

**摘要:** VBVR-Pro是一个原生视觉推理的闭环测试平台，通过300个程序生成的任务、可验证的奖励机制和跨30+生成器的控制实验，使视觉生成本身成为推理媒介。


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** PointRL: Learning Point-Level Vision-Language Grounding from Verifiable Annotation Evidence
**作者:** Jingyang Su, Pu Cao, Xiuze Jin
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25299

**相关性说明:** This paper addresses visual grounding/point localization in 2D images using VLMs, which is a computer vision task completely different from the research profile's focus on Computer Graphics - specifically 3D motion generation from video, video-to-3D transformation, and visual guidance feature extraction for motion generation. While both mention VLMs, the application domains are entirely different: the profile needs VLM for motion generation supervision and quality control, while this paper uses VLM for 2D point localization. The paper does not address extracting 3D visual guidance features, motion transformation, or motion generation quality control.

**摘要:** PointRL是一个可验证的强化学习框架，用于从现有异构标注数据中学习点级视觉定位。核心思想是将边界框、分割掩码等标注转换为点定位指令，并通过外部验证器评估预测质量。
