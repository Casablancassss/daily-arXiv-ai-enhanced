# 📊 每日 arXiv 论文推荐

**研究画像:**
- 领域: Computer Graphics
- 方法: Video Generation Models (Wanxiang/万相, Seedance), Vision-Language Models (VLM) for quality supervision, 3D Motion Generation Models, Visual Guidance Feature Extraction, Two-stage generation pipeline (2D to 3D)
- 痛点: Difficulty in generating high-quality 3D human motions from text alone, Challenge of extracting reliable 3D visual guidance features from 2D video priors, Lack of effective quality control mechanisms for generated motions, Domain gap between 2D video and 3D motion representations

## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Field Converter: Geometry-Initialized Temporal Residual Refinement for World-Grounded Player Pose Estimation from Soccer Broadcasts
**作者:** Simon Khan, Laurent Gajny, Jennyfer Lecompte
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10498

**相关性说明:** While this paper deals with 3D human pose/motion, it addresses POSE ESTIMATION (perceiving/reconstructing existing poses from video), not MOTION GENERATION (creating new motions). The research profile specifically focuses on generating 3D human motions from text/video - a generative task. Additionally, the application context differs: this paper targets sports broadcast analysis with known camera geometry and field markers, while the profile involves text-to-motion or video-to-motion generation pipelines. The methods also differ - this uses geometry initialization with temporal residual refinement, not diffusion models or video generation models mentioned in the profile.

**摘要:** Field Converter是一个用于从标定足球转播中估计世界坐标3D球员姿态的几何初始化时间残差框架，通过几何初始化和时序残差校正将根误差从49cm降低到10-11cm，世界空间MPJPE达到13.2cm。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 30%
**标题:** MotionBlind: Probing the Illusion of Motion Understanding in Video-LLMs
**作者:** Dhairya Bhatia, Bishoy Galoaa, Oliver Fritsche
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09528

**相关性说明:** This paper tests Video-LLMs' ability to understand physical motion (speed, direction, force) as a perception benchmark for world models. While it involves Video-LLMs, it does NOT address 3D human motion generation - it's purely about evaluating motion understanding capabilities, not about generating or extracting features for 3D motion. The application domain is fundamentally different: this is AI benchmark evaluation, not computer graphics motion generation. It doesn't help with text-to-motion generation, 2D-to-3D motion extraction, or the quality control mechanisms mentioned in the pain points.

**摘要:** Video-LLMs无法理解物理运动（速度、方向、力度），无法作为世界模型的感知前端。作者推出MotionBlind基准测试，通过对比仅在运动上不同的视频对来测试模型的运动理解能力，发现大多数模型（包括开源和前沿模型）都接近随机猜测水平，仅Gemini 3.1 Pro能通过基准测试，但即使是它也难以准确判断速度。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** Valerant: An Automatic Navigable Game Map Generator via Action-Conditioned World Model Exploration
**作者:** Yiran Qiao, Feng Wang, Jing Ma
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09418

**相关性说明:** This paper is about 3D GAME MAP/ENVIRONMENT generation, while the research profile is about 3D HUMAN MOTION generation. These are fundamentally different application domains within Computer Graphics. Although both involve 3D generation, the target is completely different - one generates game environments, the other generates human body movements. The methods (world models, SLAM for map building vs motion generation models) are also different. This represents a case of using similar 3D generation concepts but in entirely different application domains.

**摘要:** 本文提出Valerant框架，将预训练的动作条件世界模型转化为可用于探索和构建3D游戏地图的WAM（世界动作模型），通过结合视觉预测 rollout 与 SLAM 空间重建，实现从单张图像逐步生成可导航的3D游戏地图。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 15%
**标题:** MLLMs Hallucinate when Information Distribution Drifts in Synergy Heads
**作者:** Meng'en Qin, Junye Chen, Jucheng Liu
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09206

**相关性说明:** This paper addresses hallucination reduction in Multi-Modal Large Language Models (MLLMs) for general AI tasks, which is completely unrelated to the research profile's focus on 3D human motion generation in Computer Graphics. While both mention "Vision-Language Models," the application domains are entirely different: the paper deals with reducing hallucinations in general VLM outputs (image captioning, VQA, etc.), whereas the research profile needs VLM for quality supervision in 3D human motion generation. The methods (causal noise intervention, synergy heads) are specific to hallucination detection and are not applicable to motion generation pipelines. This is a case of same underlying method (VLM) but completely wrong application domain.

**摘要:** 本文提出HEAL方法，通过头级别信息解耦和校准来识别和减轻多模态大语言模型中的幻觉问题。


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 15%
**标题:** OmniPoint: Universal Monocular Metric Pointcloud from Any Camera
**作者:** Botao Ye, Marc Pollefeys, Ming-Hsuan Yang
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09394

**相关性说明:** This paper is about general 3D point cloud reconstruction from monocular images across different camera models, which is a completely different application domain from 3D human motion generation. While both deal with 3D reconstruction, the specific domain (general scene/point cloud reconstruction vs human motion generation) is entirely different. The research profile is specifically about generating human motions from text/video, not reconstructing general 3D scenes or point clouds. This is like comparing medical image generation to human motion generation - different application domains entirely.

**摘要:** OmniPoint是一个统一的单目3D重建框架，能够跨不同相机模型（针孔、鱼眼、全景）进行度量几何恢复，采用解耦的射线和距离表示，以及双向数据增强策略解决训练数据稀缺问题，并通过可学习的输入状态嵌入处理可选输入。
