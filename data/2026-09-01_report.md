## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 55%
**标题:** RASA: Disentangled Spatial-Motional Priors for Cross-Identity Character Animation
**作者:** Zhen Xiao, Zhen Shen, Zhaofan Qiu
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28219

**相关性说明:** This paper addresses 3D character animation in Computer Graphics using diffusion models and SMPL parameters, which is related to human motion generation. However, it focuses on cross-identity character animation (2D/3D avatar animation) rather than the specific research profile's focus on extracting 3D guidance from 2D video priors and using Video Generation Models (Wanxiang/Seedance) and VLM supervision. The application domain is similar but the specific methods and use cases differ - this paper emphasizes spatial-temporal disentanglement for character animation rather than video-to-3D motion synthesis from video priors.

**摘要:** 本文提出RASA框架，通过将空间映射与运动控制解耦来解决跨身份角色动画问题。框架包含两个阶段：空间先验校准器(SPC)融合参考身份与驱动姿态生成空间锚定的初始噪声潜在向量；内在运动引导器(IMG)将SMPL关节参数编码为语义运动向量并注入DiT中间层。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 55%
**标题:** SignRR: Retrieve and Refine Real Motion for Sign Language Production
**作者:** Fidel Omar Tito Cruz, Angie Sanchez Marquina, Summy Farfan
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28568

**相关性说明:** The paper is about sign language production, which falls under human motion generation but is a highly specialized subdomain. While it addresses some aspects like temporal consistency and motion quality, it lacks alignment with the key pain points: (1) No 2D-to-3D visual feature extraction from video priors; (2) No Vision-Language Models (VLM) for supervision; (3) Uses VQ-VAE instead of diffusion models. The method (retrieve-and-refine with Residual VQ-VAE) is fundamentally different from the specified methods (diffusion models, video generation models like Wanxiang/Seedance). It's relevant as human motion generation but not for the specific research direction outlined.

**摘要:** 提出SignRR框架，采用retrieve-and-refine范式解决手语生成问题，通过检索真实运动片段并结合残差VQ-VAE细化，实现全局连贯的连续手语生成。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 30%
**标题:** GraspHOI: Full-Body 3D Human-Object Reconstruction with Finger-Level Grasps from a Single In-the-Wild Image
**作者:** Semin Kim, Haechan Shin, Jongyoo Kim
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28386

**相关性说明:** This paper is about 3D human-object reconstruction from a single image (static scene reconstruction), while the research profile is about 3D human MOTION GENERATION (temporal synthesis). Although both are in the broader 3D human analysis domain, the application domains are fundamentally different: the profile seeks video/motion generation methods, while this paper addresses static 3D pose/scene reconstruction. The paper doesn't address the pain points of extracting features from video priors, bridging 2D-to-3D for motion generation, VLM-based video supervision, or temporal consistency. Methods also differ - no diffusion models or video generation techniques involved.

**摘要:** GraspHOI是首个从单张图像重建完整人体-物体3D交互的框架，同时显式优化手指关节以匹配重建的物体几何，无需预定义网格或固定类别词汇表。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** VidParse: Online Parsing of Egocentric Procedures Like a Pro
**作者:** Anubhav Gupta, Archit Kambhamettu, Vatsal Agarwal
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27562

**相关性说明:** This paper addresses egocentric video action segmentation/parsing, which is fundamentally different from 3D Human Motion Generation. While it uses frozen foundation models for visual feature extraction, it's about UNDERSTANDING/segmenting existing video actions (action recognition), not GENERATING new 3D motion. The application domain is completely different - motion recognition vs motion synthesis. It doesn't address any of the specified pain points: extracting 3D guidance from 2D, bridging 2D-3D domain gap, VLM-based quality supervision, or motion synthesis. Similar use of visual feature extraction but wrong application domain.

**摘要:** VidParse是一个在线、无需训练的框架,用于将连续的自我中心视频流解析为离散的时间顺序动作步骤。该方法通过图约束推理解决视觉挑战,使用基于冻结基础模型的 manipulation-anchored 特征构建时间相似性矩阵,并通过束搜索解码器利用任务图强制执行有效的动作转换。


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** Cyc3D: Evaluating Cyclic Structural Stability and Asset Usability in Image-to-3D Generation
**作者:** Liwen Zhang
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28080

**相关性说明:** This paper is about Image-to-3D generation (creating 3D object assets from single images), which is a completely different application domain from 3D Human Motion Generation (animating human movements/skeletal motion). While both involve "3D generation" in computer graphics, they are fundamentally different: one creates static 3D objects/assets, the other generates temporal human motion sequences. The methods and pain points (temporal consistency in motion, video-to-motion extraction) do not align with this image-to-3D object evaluation benchmark paper.

**摘要:** Cyc3D是一个多维基准测试，用于评估图像到3D生成任务，侧重于跨视图对象一致性和表征质量，而非仅评估渲染视图的 plausibility 和语义对齐。该基准通过闭环的render-regenerate-align协议量化几何、感知和语义漂移，并评估网格离散化效率和UV参数化质量。实验表明封闭源代码的前馈模型优于开源优化基线，但即使最强方法 cycle-stability 分数仍低于48。
