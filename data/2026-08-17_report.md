## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 45%
**标题:** Marionette: Predicting World States, Rendering Geometry, Painting Appearance
**作者:** Zian Meng, Zhen Li, Chuanhao Li
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14530

**相关性说明:** This paper focuses on game world modeling/simulation (predicting 3D world states for interactive games), not on 3D motion generation from 2D video priors. While it uses similar elements (3D skeleton/pose representation, video diffusion), the application domain is fundamentally different - it's about game graphics and character control in virtual environments, not about extracting or generating 3D human motion from visual inputs. The pain points addressed (long-term consistency, controllability in games) differ from the research profile's focus on bridging 2D video to 3D motion generation.

**摘要:** Marionette是一个用于交互式游戏的世界模型,通过显式建模3D世界状态(276维关节骨架、根轨迹、旋转),将几何计算委托给零参数渲染器,仅用神经网络合成外观,实现长期一致性、可控性和可修复性。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 30%
**标题:** ForgeWM: Progressive Causal Training for Few-Step Action-Conditioned Video World Models
**作者:** Xinye Li, Lingshuai Lin, Lei Wang
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14022

**相关性说明:** This paper is about action-conditioned video world models for games (Minecraft, FPS), which generates 2D video frames from actions. While it uses diffusion models similar to the research profile's methods, the APPLICATION DOMAIN is fundamentally different - it's about game video generation, not 3D motion generation from 2D video priors. The research profile focuses on bridging 2D visual features to 3D motion representations for motion synthesis, while this paper focuses on low-latency video generation for game control. This is a case of using similar methods (diffusion) in completely different application domains.

**摘要:** ForgeWM是一个渐进式框架，将双向动作条件视频生成器通过领域适应、强制因果训练、因果一致性蒸馏和策略分布匹配转换为高效的几步世界模型，支持1/2/4步去噪预算和双路径部署（延迟关键交互+重放时细化），在Minecraft和FPS游戏中实现领先的视频生成质量。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 30%
**标题:** SPARGen: Unifying Spatial Perception and Reasoning through Native Multimodal Generation
**作者:** Jinsheng Quan, Jianhua Li, Siyi Xie
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14138

**相关性说明:** This paper addresses 3D scene reconstruction, dense correspondence, and spatial reasoning - which is a DIFFERENT application domain from the research profile's focus on 3D MOTION generation from 2D video priors. While SPARGen uses native multimodal generation (which is somewhat related to VLM approaches), it targets static spatial perception tasks rather than temporal motion synthesis. The pain points are also misaligned: SPARGen focuses on spatial reasoning and 3D reconstruction, not on quality control of 2D video priors for motion generation, bridging domain gap for motion representations, or ensuring temporal consistency in synthesized motions. This is essentially a different subfield within computer graphics - 3D scene understanding vs. 3D motion generation.

**摘要:** SPARGen是一个统一的多模态框架，将3D重建、密集对应和空间推理统一为指令条件生成任务，实现单一原生多模态生成模型处理多种空间感知任务。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** ChartProbe: A Diagnostic Study on Visual Reasoning through Perception, Grounding, and Simple Reasoning
**作者:** Mahsa Khoshnoodi, Sarah Adel Bargal
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13766

**相关性说明:** The paper is about visual reasoning for chart/diagram question answering using VLMs, while the research profile is about Computer Graphics for 3D motion generation from 2D video priors. Although both involve Vision Language Models, the application domains are completely different: chart reasoning vs. video-to-3D motion synthesis. The paper doesn't address any of the stated pain points (2D-to-3D conversion, temporal consistency, domain gap between 2D visual features and 3D motion).

**摘要:** 本文提出ChartProbe诊断框架，质疑视觉语言模型在图表推理任务中的困难是否真正源于"复杂推理能力不足"，认为问题可能在于更基础的感知、定位和简单推理技能。通过对三种开源VLM的实验，仅在单一简单技能上进行微调，就能大幅提升模型在未训练过的复杂推理问题上的表现，且泛化到分布外场景。


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 25%

**作者:** Jialong Guo, Ke Liu, Mengxuan Li

**相关性说明:** This paper is about neural video representation and encoding (NeRV-style methods), which is a completely different application domain from 3D motion generation. While both deal with video, the research profile is about extracting 2D video features for 3D motion synthesis, whereas this paper focuses on efficient video tokenization and reconstruction. The application domains are fundamentally different - one is about generating 3D human/robot motion, the other is about representing/compressing video data. Using similar neural network concepts (tokens, encoding) doesn't make them relevant to each other in terms of actual application.

**摘要:** CoANeRV是一个坐标感知的token空间框架，通过前馈传递形成视频token并使用共享解码器重建，实现高效的amortized video representation
