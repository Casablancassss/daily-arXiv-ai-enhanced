- 痛点: Extracting reliable visual guidance features from 2D video priors, Maintaining motion fidelity when converting 2D visual information to 3D space, Quality supervision and filtering of generated videos before motion extraction, Bridging the domain gap between 2D video and high-quality 3D motion synthesis

## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 45%
**标题:** DreamHand: Repurposing Video Diffusion Models for Occlusion-Robust Egocentric 3D Hand Motion Recovery
**作者:** Yufei Liu, Xixi Wang, Hao Li
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20308

**相关性说明:** The paper addresses 3D hand motion from egocentric video, which tangentially relates to the 3D human motion domain. However, it focuses on MOTION RECOVERY/ESTIMATION from video (inverse problem), not TEXT-TO-MOTION GENERATION (forward problem). While it uses video diffusion models and addresses occlusion/out-of-view challenges, the core task differs fundamentally: extracting motion from existing video versus generating motion from text. This is more related to computer vision pose estimation than computer graphics motion synthesis. The methods (deterministic encoding for recovery) differ from the profile's focus on video generation models for motion synthesis.

**摘要:** DreamHand是一种从自我中心视频中恢复度量级3D双手轨迹的离线片段级框架，通过将视频扩散模型重新用途为确定性几何编码器，在5个基准数据集上实现新的最优性能，在遮挡严重和视野外场景中表现尤为突出。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Stream4D: 4D-Consistency for Streaming Autoregressive Diffusion Video Models
**作者:** Yuanhao Ban, Jiaqi Feng, Hengguang Zhou
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19556

**相关性说明:** This paper addresses video generation consistency (4D reconstruction, geometric drift) but for general video/scene generation, not specifically for 3D human motion generation. While it uses diffusion models similar to methods in the research profile (like Wanxiang, Seedance), the application domain is fundamentally different. The research profile focuses on Text-to-Motion/3D human motion synthesis from visual inputs, whereas this paper focuses on maintaining temporal-spatial consistency in general video generation. The pain points differ significantly - this paper addresses geometric drift in video generation, not the 2D-to-3D human motion conversion or visual guidance extraction challenges in the profile.

**摘要:** Stream4D提出了一种用于流式自回归视频扩散模型的训练方法，通过引入前馈4D重建奖励和运动先验来解决长时序视频生成中的几何漂移和静态化问题，取代了之前使用静态3D Gaussian-Splatting作为批评者的方法。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** AvatarDynamizer: From Static to Dynamic Human Avatars via Generative Dynamic Textures
**作者:** Guoxing Sun, Heming Zhu, Linjie Lyu
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19900

**相关性说明:** While this paper deals with 3D human avatars and motion, it focuses on a DIFFERENT application domain - converting static 3D avatars to dynamic ones with surface dynamics (clothing wrinkles, skin deformation). The research profile is specifically about Text-to-Motion / 3D Human Motion Generation from video priors. AvatarDynamizer is about avatar rendering and surface dynamics, not text-to-motion generation or extracting motion from video. The methods (texture space dynamics, 3D Gaussians) and goals (surface dynamics vs skeletal motion generation) are different from the specified research profile about video-to-motion conversion and quality supervision.

**摘要:** AvatarDynamizer是一个生成式方法,可以将现成的静态3D头像转换为可控制的、逼真的且多视角一致的四维头像。该方法通过引入纹理空间表面动力学嵌入,将头像动力学建模为条件纹理生成,并使用编码器-解码器表示将姿态依赖的动力学嵌入动态纹理图,再解码为3D高斯进行多视角一致渲染。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** 4DAnyone: Create Anyone in 4D from a Casual Monocular Video
**作者:** Yudong Jin, Tao Xie, Qihang Zhang
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20335

**相关性说明:** The paper addresses 4D human reconstruction from monocular video, which is DIFFERENT from the research profile's focus on Text-to-Motion / 3D Human Motion Generation. While both deal with humans in computer graphics, 4DAnyone is about reconstructing 4D Gaussian Splatting representations from video (reconstruction/generation from existing video), not about generating NEW motions from text. The application domains are fundamentally different: reconstruction vs. text-to-motion synthesis. The paper addresses multi-view video consistency but does not address text-to-motion conversion, VLM quality supervision, or downstream motion generation pipelines mentioned in the profile.

**摘要:** 4DAnyone是一个从无标定单目视频重建4D人体的框架，通过生成重建级的多视角一致视频并将其提升到4D高斯溅射(4DGS)。该方法解决了现有相机控制视频扩散模型在扩展到数十个目标视角时无法保持一致性的问题。


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** VideoRun2D Demo: Markerless Body Tracking for Biomechanical Analysis of Running
**作者:** Luis F. Gomez, Julian Fierrez, Roberto Daza
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19480

**相关性说明:** This paper focuses on markerless 2D pose estimation for biomechanical analysis of running (sports science), which is fundamentally different from the target field of Text-to-Motion / 3D Human Motion Generation in Computer Graphics. While it deals with extracting visual features from running videos, it uses traditional pose estimation methods (not video generation models like Wanxiang/Seedance) and only tracks 2D joint angles without generating 3D motion. It addresses none of the specified pain points about 2D-to-3D conversion, quality supervision, or domain gap bridging for motion synthesis.

**摘要:** VideoRun2D是一个用于短跑生物力学分析的视频框架，使用不同的人体姿态估计器追踪314段专业运动员跑步视频中的髋关节和膝关节角度，平均RMSE范围为11.46°至5.83°，结合后处理模块可降至9.87°和5.30°。
