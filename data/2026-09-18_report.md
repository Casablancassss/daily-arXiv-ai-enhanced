**标题:** Track, Articulate, Act: Generating Articulation from Casual Human Videos
**作者:** Jiaming Zhang, Homanga Bharadhwaj
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19119

**相关性说明:** This paper addresses some pain points from the research profile (extracting 3D motion from 2D video, bridging 2D-3D gap) and is in Computer Graphics domain. However, there's a key application domain mismatch: the research profile focuses on 3D motion generation (typically human motion), while this paper focuses on articulated object reconstruction (doors, drawers, hand-object interaction) and hand motion. The methods also differ - the profile mentions Video Generation Models and VLMs, while this paper uses 3D reconstruction, mesh segmentation, and scene flow models. While both deal with 3D from 2D, the specific application (articulated objects vs human motion generation) makes this only moderately relevant.

**摘要:** 本文提出了一个real-to-sim框架，能够从单目RGB视频中重建仿真的铰接物体和手-物体交互，无需RGB-D、多视角输入、先前扫描、手动指定关节或机器人演示。核心是利用密集3D点轨迹作为与具体载体无关的铰接线索：固定连杆上的点保持静止，而运动连杆上的点遵循一致的旋转或平移运动。
