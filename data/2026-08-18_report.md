**相关性说明:** This paper is highly relevant as it addresses the SAME application domain (3D human motion generation/editing in Computer Graphics). **摘要:** 本文提出CIME框架，用于解决文本驱动的人体运动编辑问题，通过在空间姿态和时间节奏维度上解耦变化与不变性，实现高质量的运动编辑。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 55%
**标题:** HOIMask: Towards Generative Masked Modeling for Human Object Interaction Generation
**作者:** Yihong Ji, Jinsong Zhang, He Hu
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15141

**相关性说明:** The paper addresses HOI (Human-Object Interaction) motion generation within Computer Graphics, which is in the same general application domain as the research profile (3D motion generation). However, there are significant mismatches: (1) The profile specifically requires diffusion-based methods, but this paper uses masked modeling with VQ - a different generation paradigm; (2) The paper doesn't address the pain points about VLM-based quality supervision or extracting 3D guidance from 2D video; (3) While it does address physical plausibility and temporal consistency (mentioned in profile), these are secondary concerns. The core method (masked modeling vs diffusion) and focus (HOI-specific vs general text-to-motion) create moderate relevance.

**摘要:** HOIMask是一种用于人-物交互(HOI)运动生成的生成式掩码框架，首次在离散空间中建模HOI运动。该方法通过HOI矢量量化(VQ)将运动序列和接触感知信号编码为离散的2D人类和物体标记地图，并使用Transformer架构来捕捉复杂的时空和交互依赖关系。在推理过程中引入接触感知重建指导来生成更连贯、物理上更合理的运动。
