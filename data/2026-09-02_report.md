## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 45%
**标题:** Everybody Tracking Every Body
**作者:** Daeyun Shin, Yunhan Zhao, Shu Kong
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29927

**相关性说明:** This paper addresses 3D pose estimation/tracking from egocentric cameras using diffusion models, while the research profile focuses on 3D motion GENERATION from video priors. Both involve human motion and diffusion models, but they are different application domains: pose estimation vs motion generation. The paper does touch on 2D-3D bridging and multi-modal fusion (similar methods), but the core task (estimating existing poses vs generating new motions) does not directly address the pain points related to video-to-motion generation pipelines, VLM-based motion filtering, or quality supervision of generated motions.

**摘要:** 本文研究从多人体佩戴的 ego-centric 相机视角进行3D人体姿态估计的问题。提出一种扩散模型方法，将基于头部运动的姿态估计与外视角姿态观测进行融合。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Elastic Triangle Splatting
**作者:** Tian Shi, Shenhan Qian, Daniel Cremers
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29106

**相关性说明:** This paper is about triangle splatting for novel view synthesis and shape reconstruction, which is a different application domain from the research profile's focus on 3D motion generation from video priors. While both are in Computer Graphics, this paper deals with 3D scene rendering/view synthesis, not motion generation. The methods (triangle splatting, kernel optimization) are unrelated to the profile's methods (video generation models, VLM, motion generation). The paper addresses 3D reconstruction of static scenes rather than temporal motion generation, making it only marginally relevant as it touches on 3D representation in computer graphics but doesn't address the specific pain points of video-to-motion generation.

**摘要:** 本文研究了三角形splatting技术中的核函数设计，提出了一种弹性核函数，通过双边梯度支持和自适应边界值来改进形状重建和新型视图合成的优化效果。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Dancing Stick Figures: An Introductory Dataset for Training Video Generation Models
**作者:** Jin Hyuk Cho
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29123

**相关性说明:** This paper focuses on 2D video generation (dancing stick figures) rather than 3D motion generation. While it provides ground truth joint positions and uses synthetic video data, the application domain is fundamentally different from the research profile's goal of using video priors for 3D motion generation with VLM supervision. The paper addresses video generation training difficulties, not the pain points of bridging 2D visual features to 3D motion space or quality supervision using VLM.

**摘要:** Dancing Stick Figures是一个针对视频生成模型训练困难问题而构建的合成视频数据集。该数据集具有64x64分辨率、64帧的视频，包含4,020个视频片段（1,340个六秒源动作，每个从3个摄像机渲染），总大小0.79GB，可用于在单个工作站GPU上进行实用重复训练。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Biomechanical 3D Body: Self-Supervised Distillation of Biomechanical Pose from a 3D Body Foundation Model
**作者:** R. James Cotton, J. D. Peiffer, Lucinda Williamson
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29928

**相关性说明:** This paper focuses on 3D body pose ESTIMATION (recovering biomechanical joint angles from images) rather than 3D motion GENERATION. While both deal with human bodies, they are different application domains: the profile is about synthesizing new motions from video priors, while this paper is about analyzing/reconstructing poses from single images. The methods are also completely different - this paper uses SAM-3D-Body, Levenberg-Marquardt IK solver, and MuJoCo biomechanical models, whereas the profile requires video generation models (万相, Seedance), VLMs, and multi-modal fusion pipelines. No overlap in methods or application goals.

**摘要:** 本文提出了一种从单张RGB图像预测生物力学模型关节角度和尺度的深度学习框架，通过在SAM-3D-Body基础上添加生物力学预测头，并利用基于Levenberg-Marquardt逆运动学求解器的闭环优化进行监督，实现了知识蒸馏，可在无标注图像上训练。


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Lucida: Parse, Generate, and Place for Composable Real-to-Sim Scene Modeling
**作者:** Minghan Qin, Yuang Wang, Xiuyu Yang
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30821

**相关性说明:** While both papers deal with Computer Graphics and use VLMs, they address completely different application domains. The research profile focuses on 3D MOTION GENERATION from 2D video priors (animating motion over time), while Lucida focuses on REAL-TO-SIM SCENE MODELING (static object placement and scene reconstruction). The paper uses VLM for GUI-based object placement tasks, not for motion quality supervision or generation. There is no mention of video generation models (万相, Seedance), 3D motion generation, or bridging 2D visual features to 3D motion space - all core pain points in the profile. The methods (scene parsing, asset generation, object placement) are unrelated to motion generation pipelines.

**摘要:** Lucida是一种可组合场景建模方法，通过重新分配任务需求，使每一步仅依赖真实捕获可可靠提供的输入，在管道末端达到精度而非在起点要求。包含场景图解析、资产生成和GizmoAct VLM策略放置三个模块。
