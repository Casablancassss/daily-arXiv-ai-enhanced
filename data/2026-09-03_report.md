## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** CQF-HMR: Continuous Quaternion Flows for Probabilistic 3D Human Mesh Recovery from a Single Image
**作者:** Cuong Le, Bao-Long Tran, Pavlo Melnyk
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00995

**相关性说明:** This paper addresses 3D Human Mesh Recovery from a single image using quaternion-constrained continuous normalizing flows, which is a different application domain from Text-to-Motion Generation. The research profile focuses on generating motion from text/video (e.g., using Video Generation Models like Wanxiang/Seedance, VLM for quality supervision), while this paper focuses on estimating/recovering 3D human pose from a static image. While both deal with 3D human representation, mesh recovery is a perception/estimation task, not a generation task. The methods also differ significantly - this paper uses normalizing flows for pose estimation rather than video generation or text-to-motion synthesis methods.

**摘要:** 本文提出了一种基于四元数约束的连续归一化流方法，用于从单张2D图像中概率化地恢复3D人体模型。该方法解决了3D关键点方法产生不合理姿态的问题，同时克服了SMPL方法中关节旋转非加性带来的建模复杂性。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Seeing the World and the Self from Egocentric Video
**作者:** Kai Guan, Minchao Jiang, Ruichen WangLi
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01276

**相关性说明:** The paper addresses 3D motion synthesis from video (egocentric video) but the application domain is VIDEO-TO-MOTION, not TEXT-TO-MOTION as required. While it uses diffusion models for motion generation (method similarity), it lacks text input and does not address VLM-based quality supervision/filtering. It partially addresses "3D motion synthesis from 2D video priors" pain point but misses the core text-to-motion generation focus and visual guidance feature extraction from video generation models.

**摘要:** RESELF是一个统一框架，通过将确定性度量几何重建与几何条件运动生成相结合，从自我中心视频中同时恢复场景和穿戴者的全身运动。该方法利用几何基础模型结合扩散模型，并引入闭环运动反馈阶段优化相机姿态。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** Beyond Language Priors: Diagnosing and Fixing Visual-Origin Hallucinations in Multimodal LLM
**作者:** Peiyang Xu, Xiaopei Zhu, Jun Zhu
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00231

**相关性说明:** This paper focuses on Multimodal LLM hallucination reduction (visual-origin hallucinations in image-text models), which is a completely different application domain from Text-to-Motion Generation. While both involve visual feature extraction and VLM concepts, this paper addresses hallucination detection/correction in general MLLMs, not motion synthesis from 2D video to 3D motion. The methods (ACFT for hallucination reduction) and end goal (reducing false object predictions in MLLMs) are unrelated to the research profile's goal of bridging 2D video generation with 3D motion generation.

**摘要:** 本文挑战了将多模态大语言模型(MLLM)对象幻觉仅归因于语言先验的传统观点，提出视觉起源幻觉作为补充原因，并通过对抗对比微调(ACFT)方法解决该问题，仅使用0.9%的COCO数据即在多个基准上取得SOTA效果。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** FoldingAgent: Inferring Parametric Origami Procedures from Demonstration Videos
**作者:** Maya Moriya, Sigal Raab, Yael Vinker
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00377

**相关性说明:** This paper is about using VLM to infer origami folding procedures from demonstration videos - it's in the domain of geometric reasoning and procedure inference from video, NOT Text-to-Motion Generation. While it does use VLM similar to the research profile, the application domain is completely different (origami folding vs. 3D human motion synthesis from 2D video priors). The paper doesn't address text-to-motion generation, 3D motion synthesis, video generation models (Wanxiang/Seedance), or any of the pain points related to bridging 2D video and 3D motion generation. This is a case of using similar methods (VLM) but in a completely different application domain - origami procedure inference vs. human motion generation.

**摘要:** FoldingAgent是一个从 origami 演示视频中推断显式参数化折叠程序的智能体框架。该框架利用预训练视觉语言模型(VLM)配备专用工具集,能够模拟几何转换、验证物理合理性、检索比较视觉内容并评估自身预测。


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** Streaming4D: Accelerate 4D World Models via Block-wise Video Generation and Incremental Reconstruction
**作者:** Xiaoyan Liu, Jiaxin Liu, Kangrui Li
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00610

**相关性说明:** This paper addresses 4D world model generation (generating dynamic 3D scenes/environments that evolve over time), which is fundamentally different from the research profile's focus on Text-to-Motion Generation (generating human/animal body motions from text). While both use video generation and 3D reconstruction techniques, the application domain is completely different - this is about scene-level 4D generation, not character motion generation. The paper does use video generation and 3D reconstruction methods, but in the wrong application domain entirely.

**摘要:** Streaming4D提出了一种紧密耦合的同步流水线，通过将分块自回归视频生成与增量3D重建相结合，解决了4D生成中交互延迟高的问题。该方法在视频块生成后立即触发重建，实现合成与几何更新的并行执行。
