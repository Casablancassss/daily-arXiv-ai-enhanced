## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 45%
**标题:** Forwardrobe: Garment-Aware Gaussian Avatars from a Single Image
**作者:** Daisheng Jin, Shuyun Wang, Ying He
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29106

**相关性说明:** Moderately relevant within Computer Graphics, but the application domain differs significantly. The research profile focuses on 3D motion generation from 2D VIDEO (temporal consistency, video-to-motion synthesis), while Forwardrobe addresses 3D AVATAR RECONSTRUCTION from a single IMAGE (static to 3D). Both involve 3D avatars/human synthesis but solve different problems: motion generation (creating new animations) vs. avatar reconstruction (creating 3D geometry from input). The methods also differ - the profile uses video generation models and VLMs, while Forwardrobe uses Gaussian splatting and non-rigid deformation. While the paper is high quality in its domain, it doesn't address the specific pain points of video-to-motion generation, temporal consistency, or VLM-based quality control.

**摘要:** Forwardrobe是一种从单张图像重建可动画3D人物化身的前馈框架，通过在高斯空间中显式分离服装与人体，并引入连续性感知几何、姿态条件非刚性变形和外观适配等技术，有效解决宽松衣物重建和动画难题，同时支持服装编辑、迁移和虚拟试穿等应用。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** FocusGS: Spatial Delta Layers for Local Repair and Deterministic Editing of Trained 3D Gaussian Assets
**作者:** Yiqun Pan, Yukun Shi
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28834

**相关性说明:** While this paper is in Computer Graphics, it addresses a completely different application domain. The research profile focuses on 3D MOTION GENERATION from 2D video using video generation models (Wanxiang, Seedance) and VLMs, while FocusGS is about local repair and editing of already-trained 3D Gaussian Splatting assets. The methods and goals are unrelated - one generates motion from video, the other edits static 3DGS models. Only the general "3D in Computer Graphics" domain overlaps slightly.

**摘要:** FocusGS是一种用于3D高斯溅射(3DGS)资产的局部修复和确定性编辑的统一框架，通过空间delta复合实现，将修复视为纯加性特殊情况，并使用擦除-插入因子化(EIF)实现确定性编辑，解决了空间梯度匮乏问题。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 30%
**标题:** FillGS: Filling Observation Gaps in 4D Gaussian Splatting via Viewpoint-Time Selection and Generative Refinement
**作者:** Takashi Otonari, Toshihiko Yamasaki
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29284

**相关性说明:** While both papers are in Computer Graphics, they address completely different application domains. The research profile focuses on 3D MOTION GENERATION from 2D video (human/animal motion synthesis), while this paper is about 4D Gaussian Splatting for scene reconstruction and view synthesis. The methods are also different: this paper uses 4DGS with viewpoint-time selection, not video generation models or VLMs for motion generation. The paper addresses quality filtering but in the context of 3D scene reconstruction, not motion generation quality control. This is a case of using similar computer graphics techniques but in a fundamentally different application domain.

**摘要:** 提出一种主动选择时空虚拟视点来改善4DGS重建的管道，通过渲染敏感度和运动感知观察密度选择虚拟视点，过滤冲突区域后微调4DGS，在多视图视频基准测试中取得一致改进。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 30%
**标题:** Scaling Properties of Text Conditioning in Visual Generation
**作者:** Zilong Chen, Chaorui Deng, Kunchang Li
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29679

**相关性说明:** This paper studies text conditioning scaling properties in visual generation (image/video generation from text), which is a completely different application domain from the research profile's focus on 3D motion generation from 2D video. While both use diffusion models, the paper addresses text-to-image synthesis, not motion generation. The methods, evaluation benchmarks (image generation benchmarks vs motion quality metrics), and core problem (text conditioning vs video-to-3D motion synthesis) are entirely different. This is a case of "same method, different application domain" - it should receive a low score as it does not address the specific pain points of extracting visual guidance from video for 3D motion generation.

**摘要:** 研究文本条件在视觉生成中的经验缩放属性，发现扩散损失与提示词中的结构化语言量相关，通过结构化提示和提示器训练提升模型在组合、推理和世界知识基准上的表现。


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** WaiT for the Signal: Simple Frequency-Aware Flow-Matching
**作者:** Krunoslav Lehman Pavasovic, Théophane Vallaeys, Stéphane Mallat
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28760

**相关性说明:** The paper is about 2D image/video generation using wavelet-aware flow-matching, while the research profile is about 3D MOTION generation from 2D video. These are completely different application domains - image generation vs 3D human motion synthesis. Although the paper mentions video generation, it refers to video classification (Kinetics-600) not 3D motion generation. The pain points in the profile (extracting visual guidance for 3D motion, quality control for 3D synthesis, temporal consistency between video and 3D motions) are not addressed. Only tangential method similarity (flow-matching) exists, but the core application is entirely different - computer graphics image generation vs 3D motion generation.

**摘要:** WaiT是一种基于小波感知的图像Transformer，通过无损小波分解将生成过程分为粗粒和细粒 bands，遵循自然频率层次结构——高频 bands 等待粗结构出现后再加入 refinement。在 ImageNet 512x512 上达到 1.43 的 pixel-space FID，2B 模型创下 1.3 的 SOTA 记录。
