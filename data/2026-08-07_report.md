## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 50%
**标题:** UniWorld-View: Large-Baseline View Synthesis via Video Diffusion Models
**作者:** Haiyang Zhou, Wangbo Yu, Chaoran Feng
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04701

**相关性说明:** The paper uses video diffusion models and addresses 3D-guided feature extraction from 2D video, which partially matches the methods. However, the APPLICATION DOMAIN is fundamentally different: this paper focuses on VIEW SYNTHESIS (generating new camera views of scenes), while the research profile is about 3D MOTION GENERATION (synthesizing human motion from visual inputs). View synthesis and motion generation are distinct sub-fields in computer graphics. While both use diffusion models, they address different problems - this paper generates new viewpoints of a scene, not motion patterns. The pain point about "bridging domain gap between 2D video and 3D motion generation" is NOT addressed here.

**摘要:** UniWorld-View是一个统一框架，用于从单目输入实现可控的大基线新视图合成。该方法将显式3D引导与生成式扩散模型相结合，通过遮挡感知的点云渲染策略解决可见性歧义，为扩散合成提供准确先验。实验在WorldScore基准和零样本NVS基准上验证了其在可控性、几何一致性和视觉保真度方面的有效性。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Multi-View Face and Gesture Animation with Dynamic Gaussians
**作者:** Alireza Javanmardi, Vippin Kumar Jeetmal, Christen Millerdurai
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04722

**相关性说明:** While both papers deal with human motion/avatar animation in computer graphics, they address fundamentally different problems. The research profile focuses on 3D MOTION GENERATION from 2D video using diffusion models and VLMs, while this paper is about avatar RECONSTRUCTION and animation from multi-view cameras using 3D Gaussian splatting. The paper uses traditional computer graphics techniques (mesh models, Gaussian splatting) rather than the diffusion-based generation methods specified in the profile. It's a different application domain - avatar rendering vs. generative motion synthesis.

**摘要:** MVFGA是一种新型多视角一致的上半身虚拟形象生成管道，通过分别建模面部和手部并与参数化上半身网格模型融合，实现了精细面部表情和手部姿势的高质量捕获，并使用3D高斯溅射实现高质量渲染。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 30%
**标题:** ContextMaster: Interactive Multi-Shot Video Creation via Fixed-Budget Sparse Context Routing
**作者:** Xu Guo, Zhengxuan Wei, Xinghui Li
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04956

**相关性说明:** This paper is about multi-shot video creation/generation in the 2D video domain, which is different from the research profile's focus on 3D motion generation from visual inputs. While both involve video generation, the application domains are fundamentally different: the profile targets bridging 2D video to 3D human motion synthesis, while this paper addresses 2D video editing/creation with context routing. The paper doesn't address 3D motion generation, motion diffusion, or the specific pain points about extracting 3D-guided features or bridging domain gaps between 2D video and 3D motion. It's a similar method (video generation with diffusion) but wrong application domain.

**摘要:** 本文提出了交互式多镜头视频创作(IMVC)任务，并推出ContextMaster统一模型。该模型通过角色感知上下文表示、固定预算稀疏路由和ConstraintSink机制，在保持历史上下文的同时实现高效推理。提出两阶段特权上下文蒸馏框架解决稀疏上下文访问和少步推理的挑战，在三项任务上超越专用基线，达到16 FPS。

## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** Poly-OPD: Heterogeneous Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation for Capability-Selectable Flow Models
**作者:** Siming Fu, Haojun Xu, Ruizhe He
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04349

**相关性说明:** The paper focuses on TEXT-TO-IMAGE generation using flow models and knowledge distillation, which is a completely different application domain from 3D MOTION GENERATION from 2D video priors. While both use diffusion/flow models, this is analogous to needing "diffusion for human motion generation" but getting "diffusion for image generation" - the application domains are fundamentally different. The pain points addressed (combining different image generation models' strengths) have no relation to the research profile's goals (extracting 3D-guided features from video, bridging 2D video to 3D motion generation domain gap).

**摘要:** Poly-OPD是一个将多个异构教师模型的优势整合到单一紧凑流匹配学生模型中的框架。通过像素桥接进行on-policy蒸馏，使用冻结的DINOv2空间进行跨潜空间监督，并采用梯度兼容性诊断组织适配器。在将FLUX.1-dev和Z-Image蒸馏到2.5B SD3.5-Medium学生模型后，GenEval从67.3提升至73.3，DrawBench HPSv3从9.34提升至11.35。
