# 📊 每日 arXiv 论文推荐

**研究画像:**
- 领域: Computer Graphics / Text-to-Motion
- 方法: 视频生成模型（万相、Seedance）, 视觉语言大模型（VLM）质量监督, 三维运动生成模型, 视觉引导特征提取, 多模态融合方法

## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 65%
**标题:** Kirin: Animal Motion Generation from In-the-Wild Video
**作者:** Brian Nlong Zhao, Zhuoyang Pan, James M. Rehg
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01823

**相关性说明:** 论文在计算机图形学/文本到动作生成领域，研究视觉引导的运动生成和多模态融合，与研究档案有一定相关性。然而，论文的应用领域是四足动物运动生成，而非人类动作生成，且未明确使用视频生成模型（万相、Seedance）或VLM质量监督方法。尽管如此，论文确实解决了视觉引导特征提取、从2D视频重建3D运动、多模态融合等技术痛点，在方法论层面有一定参考价值。

**摘要:** Kirin框架解决动物运动捕捉难题，通过从野生视频重建3D运动，创建了首个大型四足动物视频-文本-运动对齐数据集AiM3D，并开发了视觉引导的运动生成模型，可根据文本和图像生成多样化动物的运动，最终实现3D网格的自动绑定和动画化。

## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** ZipTok3D: High-Fidelity 3D Tokenization with Compact Token Prefixes
**作者:** Mingda Lin, Weijie Wang, Zeyu Zhang
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01740

**相关性说明:** The paper is about 3D tokenization for static object reconstruction (ShapeNet, TRELLIS objects), which is fundamentally different from the research profile's focus on Text-to-Motion generation. While both involve 3D, this paper deals with compressing and reconstructing static 3D objects, not generating dynamic human motion from text or video. The application domain mismatch is critical - this is 3D object reconstruction, not human motion generation.

**摘要:** ZipTok3D是一种用于极短token序列高保真3D重建的3D分词器，通过嵌套dropout和迭代解码实现32倍（ShapeNet）和8倍（TRELLIS）的压缩率。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** SelfLift: Accelerating Few-Step Diffusion via Self-Recovering Resolution Transition
**作者:** Tingyan Wen, Chenqian Yan, Xurui Peng
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02036

**相关性说明:** This paper is about accelerating diffusion models for 2D IMAGE generation (FLUX.2-Klein, Z-Image-Turbo are image models), while the research profile is about Text-to-Motion generation for 3D human motion. Although both use diffusion models, they are in COMPLETELY DIFFERENT application domains. The paper does not address any of the pain points: it has nothing to do with 2D-to-3D motion conversion, visual guidance for motion, multi-modal fusion for motion, or motion quality assessment. This is a classic case of "similar method (diffusion) but wrong application domain" - using diffusion for image generation vs. human motion generation are fundamentally different tasks.

**摘要:** 本文提出SelfLift框架，通过自恢复机制解决少步扩散模型中渐进式分辨率推理的分布不匹配问题，实现高效推理加速。


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 25%
**标题:** TAME: Temporal-Aware Mixture-of-Experts for Text-Video Retrieval
**作者:** Uicheol Jung, Juyoung Hong, Hojung Kwon
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02204

**相关性说明:** The paper addresses text-video retrieval (finding matching videos for text), NOT text-to-motion generation (generating 3D human motion from text/video). These are completely different application domains: retrieval vs. generation. While both involve text-video alignment, the research profile is about Computer Graphics/Text-to-Motion for motion generation, whereas this paper focuses on video search/retrieval tasks. The method (CLIP with MoE for temporal modeling) is also specific to retrieval performance, not motion generation or 3D graphics.

**摘要:** 本文提出TAME框架，通过稀疏MoE层、帧-时序token和跨时序交互聚合模块来解决CLIP模型在视频检索中缺乏时序建模的问题，实现文本-视频检索性能提升。
