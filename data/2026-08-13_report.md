## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 55%
**标题:** HSTGFormer: Hyper Spatial-Temporal Graph Transformer for 3D Human Pose Estimation
**作者:** Ruochen Li, Shuang Chen, Wenke E
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12187

**相关性说明:** Moderately relevant. The paper addresses 3D human pose estimation from monocular video, which relates to the profile's goal of bridging 2D video and 3D motion representation. However, it's specifically about pose estimation using graph transformers, not the video-to-motion synthesis pipeline with Video Generation Models and VLM quality control that the profile emphasizes. The application domain is adjacent but not identical - it's more about pose estimation than the full 3D motion synthesis pipeline described in the research profile.

**摘要:** HSTGFormer是一个图增强的Transformer框架，用于单目3D人体姿态估计。它通过超空间-时间图将全局空间-时间推理分解为局部感受野，自适应双尺度时间图捕捉短程和长时间依赖，解决了现有方法中空间-时间推理分离导致的信息损失问题。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 45%
**标题:** LiveAnimate: Stable Long-Form Streaming Human Animation in Real-Time
**作者:** Yuxuan Zhang, Haozhong Xiong, Yubo Huang
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11745

**相关性说明:** Moderately relevant. Both are in the broader Computer Graphics human animation domain, and both involve video diffusion models. However, there's a fundamental mismatch: the research profile focuses on video-to-motion extraction (extracting 3D motion from 2D video), while this paper is about pose-to-video generation (generating animation from pose sequences). This is essentially the reverse direction. The paper addresses real-time streaming and stability in human animation generation, not the extraction of 3D guidance features from video priors or bridging 2D video to 3D motion representation as specified in the pain points.

**摘要:** LiveAnimate是首个结合实时流式生成与稳定长时序生成的人体动画系统，基于140亿参数的视频扩散Transformer (DiT)，在两块NVIDIA H100 GPU上实现19.63 FPS的流式推理，能够在3分钟基准测试中保持几乎恒定的感知质量和身份一致性（IQA从4.047到4.026），而先前系统会显著降质或需要数小时离线计算。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Motion-as-Prompt: Enhancing Motion Reasoning in Multimodal Large Language Models via Motion-Guided Cross-Frame Visual Prompting
**作者:** Xikai Sun, Kebin Liu, Haotian Wang
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11655

**相关性说明:** The paper deals with motion understanding/reasoning in videos using trajectories, which superficially overlaps with motion-related methods. However, this paper focuses on VIDEO UNDERSTANDING and motion reasoning in multimodal LLMs (an AI/Vision task), NOT on 3D MOTION SYNTHESIS or Computer Graphics. The research profile explicitly requires "3D motion synthesis from video-to-motion pipeline" and "extracting 3D visual guidance features from 2D video priors" - this paper is about understanding motion in videos, not generating 3D motion. This is a case of using similar concepts (motion, trajectories) but in a completely different application domain (video understanding vs 3D motion synthesis).

**摘要:** 本文提出Motion-as-Prompt (MaP)框架，通过恢复密集点轨迹、选择运动信息帧并将轨迹标记在视觉输入上，解决多模态大语言模型在视频推理中因稀疏均匀采样而丢失关键帧间过渡信息的问题，从而提升运动中心视频推理能力。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Avatar-Forever: Decoupled Parallel Training for High-Quality Real-Time Infinite Avatars
**作者:** Ruibin Li, Tao Yang, Zhiyuan Ma
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12107

**相关性说明:** This paper focuses on digital avatar video generation (2D/2.5D output) rather than 3D motion synthesis. While both involve human motion, they are different application domains: the profile targets video-to-motion pipeline for 3D motion generation, while Avatar-Forever deals with audio-driven avatar video synthesis. The paper uses a 22B video generation model but doesn't address extracting 3D visual guidance features from 2D video priors or bridging the 2D-3D domain gap. It addresses quality control for long-horizon generation but uses different methods (video generation foundation model) than those specified in the profile (VLM, 3D Motion Generation Models).

**摘要:** Avatar-Forever是一个解耦的并行训练框架，用于高质量实时无限交互数字人生成。该方法将生成效率和长 horizon 鲁棒性分开训练，避免了传统蒸馏管道中早期阶段失败影响后续优化的问题。


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** StateFlow: Building, Evolving, and Accessing 3D World States for Previsualization
**作者:** Yuyang Yin, Zixiang Li, Longxuan Deng
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12314

**相关性说明:** The paper is about 3D world/scene generation for previsualization (video creation and game prototyping), while the research profile focuses on video-to-motion synthesis (extracting 3D human motion from 2D video). These are different application domains within Computer Graphics. The paper does address 2D-to-3D bridging and uses some visual feedback, but it does not address motion synthesis, video-to-motion pipelines, or VLM-based motion quality control. It's a scene generation paper, not a motion generation paper.

**摘要:** StateFlow是一个用于生成式预可视化的以状态为中心的框架，使用可编辑的3D世界作为明确且持久的 工作状态，通过状态构建、状态演化和状态访问三个阶段来组织场景结构、演进和摄像机，支持迭代编辑并生成高质量的3D世界用于视频创作和游戏式原型开发。
