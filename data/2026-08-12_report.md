## 📄 论文 1 - 相关性: 55%
**标题:** DSAR: Dual-Stream Autoregressive Modeling of Temporal Cloth Dynamics for Photorealistic Animatable Avatars
**作者:** Haozhong Xiong, Yao Yu, Yu Zhou
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10500

**相关性说明:** The paper is in the correct domain (Computer Graphics - avatar creation from RGB video) and addresses related pain points about bridging 2D video to 3D avatar motion. However, it uses fundamentally different methods: dual-stream autoregressive framework with geometric/state flows for cloth dynamics, rather than the specified methods (Video Generation Models like Seedance, VLMs, quality assessment pipelines). The paper focuses on temporal cloth physics modeling rather than the 2D-3D feature extraction and VLM-based quality supervision approach outlined in the research profile.

**摘要:** 本文提出一种双流自回归框架，用于从RGB视频创建具有真实布料动态的拟真可动画人类avatar。该方法显式建模了布料物理中的时序因果关系，通过几何流传播表面位移，状态流融合历史状态，解决了现有方法忽略时序因果性导致的过度平滑和分布外姿态伪影问题。


## 📄 论文 2 - 相关性: 45%
**标题:** Beyond Pixels: From Video Priors to 4D Worlds
**作者:** Zihao Liu, Xiaolong Shen, Zhenglin Zhou
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10744

**相关性说明:** The paper addresses related pain points (extracting 3D guidance from 2D video, bridging 2D-3D gap) and uses similar methods (video diffusion models including Wanxiang/Seedance). However, there is a critical application domain mismatch: the research profile focuses on 3D HUMAN MOTION synthesis, while this paper focuses on 4D SCENE generation (dynamic 3D environments). These are different sub-fields within Computer Graphics - the paper generates scene dynamics, not human/animal motion. The methods are similar but the application domain differs significantly.

**摘要:** 本文提出Latent-to-4D方法，利用视频扩散模型的潜在表示直接生成4D动态3D场景，通过将视频潜在与4D解码器的token grid对齐并经由帧级和全局时空注意力细化，实现跨不同视频扩散变换器的可迁移4D预测。


## 📄 论文 3 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Visual Geometry Foundation-Aware Gaussians for Single-Frame Surround-View Driving Reconstruction
**作者:** Junhong Lin, Jinlong Wang, Xianda Guo
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10682

**相关性说明:** This paper addresses 3D reconstruction for autonomous driving using 3D Gaussian Splatting, which differs significantly from the research profile's focus on 3D MOTION generation from 2D video. While both deal with 3D visual features, this paper focuses on static scene reconstruction (not motion synthesis), uses VGGT rather than VLM/diffusion models, and targets driving perception rather than computer graphics motion synthesis. The application domain mismatch is critical: this is autonomous driving perception, not computer graphics motion generation.

**摘要:** VGGD是一个用于前向环视驾驶重建的视觉几何基础感知3D高斯溅射框架，通过利用预训练的视觉几何先验来增强上游表示并缓解稀疏环视视图中的几何歧义。


## 📄 论文 4 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Self-Geometry: GT-Free and Plug-and-Play Test-Time Adaptation for Geometrically Consistent 3D Vision Foundation Models
**作者:** Seokhyun Youn, Dahyeon Kye, Sung-Ho Bae
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10708

**相关性说明:** The paper focuses on 3D vision foundation models for geometric tasks (depth estimation, camera pose, point clouds) - this is computer vision/3D reconstruction, NOT computer graphics motion synthesis. While both are 3D-related, the application domain is fundamentally different. The research profile is about 3D motion synthesis from 2D video priors and video generation, whereas this paper is about improving VFM geometric consistency through test-time adaptation. The methods (geometric constraints, test-time adaptation) don't align with the profile's focus on video generation models, VLMs for quality assessment, or 3D motion generation.

**摘要:** 本文提出Self-Geometry，一个无需训练的视觉基础模型(VFM)测试时适应框架，通过2D像素对应关系作为伪标签，直接施加显式多视角几何约束，解决VFM预测中缺乏几何一致性的问题。


## 📄 论文 5 - 相关性: 35%
**标题:** Capturing Uncertainty in Human Motion for Representation Learning in Soccer
**作者:** Yizhou Xu, Lars Bretzner, Tiesheng Wang
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11203

**相关性说明:** While this paper deals with human motion (which is in the computer graphics domain), it focuses on soccer motion prediction using 3D skeletal data, not on the core challenges in the research profile. It doesn't address: (1) extracting 3D features from 2D video - the paper uses pre-captured 3D skeletal data; (2) Vision Language Models for quality supervision; (3) synthesizing 3D motion from 2D video priors. The method is about representation learning for prediction, not about video-to-3D motion generation. The application domain is similar (human motion) but the approach and goals are fundamentally different from what's needed here.

**摘要:** 本文提出了一种基于自监督表示学习的框架，用于理解足球运动中的3D骨骼人体动作，以未来动作预测作为学习目标。该方法通过建模离散化未来动作的概率分布来处理动作的多模态性，在大规模足球球员跟踪数据上显著提升了预测精度，并展现出良好的跨任务迁移能力。
